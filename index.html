01  /  plugins

dgtl-marketing
for Grok Bot.

Ask Grok about traffic, queries, and tags using accounts you already own. The free plugin talks to Google on your computer. DGTL does not keep the report bytes.

Ships free

Local Google reads

You sign in with your own Google OAuth. Tokens stay on the machine running Grok Bot.

  • GA4 properties you can already access
  • Search Console sites you verify
  • Google Tag Manager containers
  • Google Business Profile, feature-flagged
Paid DGTL later

Ads reads, by subscription

Google Ads and Meta Ads reads will run through an allowlisted DGTL gateway that attaches credentials and returns the response. We do not store those report bytes either.

  • Google Ads, read only
  • Meta Ads, read only
  • Priced as a DGTL subscription when it ships
Out of v1

What it will not do

v1 does not write to any ad account, tag, or property. If you need a change shipped, that is consulting.

  • No writes of any kind
  • No TikTok, Shopify, or HubSpot
  • No DGTL-hosted copy of your reports
