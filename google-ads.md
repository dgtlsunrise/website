# Google Ads · DGTL Sunrise

Pro connects Google Ads to your agent through a hosted gateway. Reporting is live. Confirm-gated manage and create are live. Creates default PAUSED. No autopilot. Full HTML: [/google-ads](/google-ads).

## Summary

- Intended for marketing operators who already administer the Google Ads accounts they connect.
- Customers connect their own Google Ads login. That path is separate from free Analytics, Search Console, and Tag Manager.
- The developer token stays on the Worker, not on the user’s machine.
- Reads use closed recipes. Live writes need your confirm with the customer id in that chat turn.
- Campaign types covered for report and create include Search, Display, Performance Max, Shopping, Video, Demand Gen, App, and Hotel (where enabled).

Install the plugin from the [homepage](/). Pro is $19 / month for the Ads and Meta gateway.
